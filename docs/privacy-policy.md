# FaithDate Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 29, 2026

App Store privacy policy URL:
`https://christianappsfamily.github.io/FaithDate/privacy-policy/`

FaithDate is a faith-based dating app prototype operated by Christian Apps Family.

## Information we collect

FaithDate may collect profile information you provide, such as name, location, faith preferences, relationship goals, photos, and messages. If advertising is enabled, advertising partners may receive device identifiers and app interaction data after any required consent.

## App tracking transparency

On iOS, FaithDate requests App Tracking Transparency permission before using data to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. You can change this permission any time in iOS Settings.

## Advertising

FaithDate may show ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may process device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, and ad interaction data according to Google's policies.

## Purchases

FaithDate offers a one-time Remove Ads in-app purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. FaithDate does not receive your full payment card details.

## How we use information

We use information to provide matching, messaging, safety, purchase, advertising, analytics, and support features.

## Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help operate the app, including app stores, advertising networks, analytics providers, and hosting providers. We may also share information when required by law or to protect users.

## Data choices

You may request deletion or correction of your account data by contacting support. You may limit tracking through iOS App Tracking Transparency settings and advertising controls on your device.
